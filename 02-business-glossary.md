# Business Glossary

This glossary defines the main business and analytical terms used throughout the project. The definitions are based primarily on the concepts and business context presented in the research.

| Term | Definition |
|---|---|
| **Accounts Receivable (AR)** | Amounts owed to the company by customers for goods or services that have been provided but have not yet been collected. |
| **Short-term Accounts Receivable** | Receivables expected to be collected within the company's normal operating cycle or within one year. |
| **Outstanding Receivables** | The remaining receivable balance that has not yet been fully collected. |
| **Overdue Receivables** | Receivable balances that remain unpaid after the agreed payment due date. |
| **Receivables Aging** | The classification of outstanding receivables based on the length of time they have remained unpaid. |
| **Order** | A transaction created when a customer requests a postal or delivery service. |
| **Shipment** | The delivery transaction associated with an order and its transportation process. |
| **Reconciliation** | The process of comparing and confirming transaction information between related parties before payment or receivable settlement. |
| **Invoice** | A document issued to record the amount payable by a customer for services provided. |
| **Due Date** | The agreed deadline by which a customer is expected to complete payment. |
| **Payment** | The settlement of an amount owed by a customer to the company. |
| **Outstanding Invoice** | An invoice that has not yet been fully paid. |
| **On-time Payment** | A payment completed on or before the agreed due date. |
| **Late Payment** | A payment completed after the agreed due date. |
| **Collection Performance** | The effectiveness of collecting outstanding receivables within the expected payment period. |
| **Days Sales Outstanding (DSO)** | An indicator used to measure the average number of days required to collect accounts receivable. |
| **Collection Effectiveness Index (CEI)** | An indicator used to evaluate the effectiveness of collecting receivables during a specific period. |
| **Overdue Receivables Rate** | The proportion of outstanding receivables that have exceeded their payment due date. |
| **Outstanding Invoice Rate** | The proportion of invoices that remain unpaid during the analysis period. |
| **Customer Payment Behavior** | The pattern of how customers fulfill their payment obligations, including payment timing and delayed payment. |
| **Accounts Receivable Concentration** | The extent to which outstanding receivables are concentrated among a limited number of customers. |
| **Cash Flow Risk** | The potential impact on cash availability resulting from delayed or uncollected receivables. |
| **Credit Term** | The agreed period or conditions within which a customer is expected to complete payment. |

## Related Business Flow

The receivables lifecycle analyzed in this project follows the business flow below:

```text
Order
  ↓
Shipment
  ↓
Reconciliation
  ↓
Invoice
  ↓
Payment
  ↓
Accounts Receivable
```

The terms in this glossary are used consistently across the project's business requirements, data model, KPI definitions, dashboard specifications, and business analysis.
