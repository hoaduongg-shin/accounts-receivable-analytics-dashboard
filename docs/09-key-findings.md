# Key Findings

## 1. Overview

The dashboard analysis was conducted using the simulated dataset developed for this project.

The findings focus on the overall receivables position, overdue debt, collection performance, customer receivables, and payment behavior.

> The results do not represent the actual financial or operational performance of any company.

---

## 2. High Level of Overdue Receivables

The analysis showed that overdue receivables accounted for **53.04% of total accounts receivable**.

This indicates that a significant proportion of receivables remained outstanding beyond the expected payment period and required further analysis at the customer and payment levels.

---

## 3. Collection Period Exceeded the Reference Threshold

The average Days Sales Outstanding (DSO) reached **28 days**, exceeding the reference credit policy threshold of approximately **25 days**.

This result indicates that the average collection period was longer than the expected payment period and reflects pressure on receivables management and cash flow.

---

## 4. Collection Performance Remained Relatively High

The Collection Effectiveness Index (CEI) reached **95.22%**.

The result shows that the overall collection process remained relatively effective. However, the high overdue receivables rate indicates that collection efficiency should be considered together with payment delays and the concentration of outstanding balances among specific customer groups.

---

## 5. Receivables Were Concentrated in Delivery Fees

Customer receivables were primarily concentrated in **Delivery fees, accounting for 95.9%** of the receivables structure.

This reflects the importance of delivery activities as the main source of revenue and receivables within the business model. :contentReference[oaicite:0]{index=0}

---

## 6. Outstanding Invoices Were Concentrated Among Specific Customers

The analysis of customers by outstanding invoice rate showed that several customers had relatively high rates of unpaid invoices, ranging from approximately **14% to 16%**.

These customers require closer monitoring to reduce the risk of prolonged receivables. :contentReference[oaicite:1]{index=1}

---

## 7. Large Customers Were a Key Source of Payment Pressure

The analysis showed that delayed payments and prolonged receivables were mainly associated with large customers and flexible credit terms.

This creates a trade-off between maintaining commercial relationships, supporting revenue growth, and controlling receivables risk. :contentReference[oaicite:2]{index=2}

---

## 8. Overall Finding

The findings show that collection performance remained relatively positive, as reflected by the CEI, while overdue debt and the collection period remained key areas requiring attention.

The dashboard supports the following analytical flow:

```text
Overall Receivables
        ↓
Overdue Debt and DSO
        ↓
Customer Contribution
        ↓
Outstanding Invoice Analysis
        ↓
Payment Behavior
```

This allows users to move from the overall receivables position to the identification of customers and payment behavior associated with overdue debt. The dashboard analysis also highlights the relationship between credit policy, customer payment behavior, and receivables risk.

## 9. Project Limitation

The findings were generated from simulated data developed for the project.

Therefore, the results demonstrate the analytical capability of the dashboard and the relationships between receivables indicators rather than the actual financial performance of a specific enterprise.
